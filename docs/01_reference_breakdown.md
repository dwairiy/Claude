## A. Reference product: ALCOI slimline system aluminium sliders

**Source:** ALCOI India Pvt. Ltd. (Panchkula, Haryana). Hero image from the article "Best System Aluminium Windows in India", June 2025.
**Asset:** `reference/product/alcoi_slimline_slider_hero.webp` (1365×2048, portrait)

### What the hero image shows
- Floor-to-ceiling sliding doors/windows with **ultra-slim matte black profiles**. The interlocks look about 20–25 mm wide.
- Large clear glass panels running wall to wall, on a nearly flush bottom track.
- A long vertical handle set into the slim interlock.
- Setting: a high-end contemporary home. Black stone floor inside, an exposed board-formed concrete courtyard wall outside, tropical planters, hard sunlight casting shadows.
- The design idea is that the boundary between inside and outside disappears.

### Product claims (from ALCOI's own copy)
| Pillar | Claim |
|---|---|
| System engineering | Factory-made modular system: frame, hardware, gasket and glazing designed together (as opposed to fabricating on site) |
| Sealing | EPDM gaskets, pressure-equalised drainage, keeps out rain, dust and drafts |
| Glazing | Single, double (DGU) or laminated glass |
| Thermal & acoustic | Thermal break plus double glazing reduce heat gain and **outside noise in metros** |
| Finish | Powder coat or anodised at 60 µm or more; matte black and woodgrain options |
| Security | Multipoint locking, anti-lift, concealed hardware, toughened/laminated glass, child restrictors |
| Experience | Testimonials stress "quieter, calmer" rooms, sliders that "open with one finger", and no leaks in the monsoon |

**Product range names to avoid in our invented brand:** Alu Series (AluFIT, AluBUILD, AluLITE), Sky Series (SkyLITE, SkyLINE, SkyWOOD).

## B. Reference commercial: AIS Windows, "Silence is a Luxury that you Deserve!"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Advertiser | Asahi India Glass Ltd (AIS Windows), uPVC & aluminium |
| Published | 26 Dec 2022, YouTube |
| Duration | **35 s** (standard 30 s TVC plus end card) |
| Format | 1920×1080, 16:9 |
| Reach | about 3.4M views |
| Campaign tags | #NoiseCancelling #BaharKaShor ("noise outside") #AbNoMore |

### Core idea in one line
A full-blown bank-heist and police-chase action sequence plays out in **total silence**. Every robber, cop and hostage holds a finger to their lips. The reveal is that we have been watching from the high-rise apartment of a woman meditating behind soundproof windows. *"No matter the noise outside, enjoy the quiet inside."*

### Structure (three acts)
| Act | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Chaos | 0–24 s (about 70%) | Comedy hook: loud genre clichés made silent with a "shh" gag |
| 2. The Reveal | 24–26 s | Overhead shot pulls back through the window glass and the POV flips inside |
| 3. The Calm and the Brand | 26–35 s | Warm interior, product benefit, voiceover, logo, end card |

### Script / voiceover (verbatim, from YouTube auto-captions)
| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 0:24.2–0:30.4 | *"No matter the noise outside, enjoy the quiet inside."* |
| 0:30.5–0:36.8 | *"With noise cancelling windows from Asahi System Windows."* |

- **No voiceover and no dialogue for the first 24 s.** The idea carries itself visually.
- The VO enters exactly on the reveal and runs over the calm act and the logo. The read is a warm, unhurried female voice, consistent with a premium-calm tone.

### Sound design and music
> **Limitation:** I could not obtain the audio track. YouTube blocks media streams from this cloud environment, and I have not used third-party mirrors. Everything below is **inferred** from the visuals, the captions and the brand description. It is not heard. To have it verified against the actual audio, drop the MP4 into `reference/ad/` and I will analyse the waveform and spectrogram, cut points and music beats precisely.

- Captions contain **no** [Music] or [Applause] tags for 0–24 s. This suggests the action plays under a near-silent bed or a music-only bed, with no diegetic gunfire, sirens or engines. That is the whole joke: *"the outside world feels like a silent film"* (brand description).
- Most likely pattern: a light, comic-tense score under the action (silent-film or heist pastiche), cut or ducked hard at the window reveal, then soft ambient pads under the VO and a sonic logo or button on the end card.

### Visual language
- **Grade:** Act 1 is desaturated and cool with teal-olive tones and a slightly faded filmic look (period crime-comedy). Act 3 is warm, golden, high-key, soft backlight. **The grade change itself tells the "outside vs inside" story.**
- **Lensing:** long lenses, shallow depth of field, mostly medium close-ups that make a frame of faces. Wides are used only for geography.
- **Wardrobe:** 90s windbreakers, beanies, aviators, moustaches, shoulder holsters. The comedy is in the deadpan cliché.
- **Casting:** strong character faces (Mediterranean or Middle Eastern look), played completely straight.
- **Edit rhythm:** about 1–2 s per shot in Act 1 (around 15 cuts in 24 s), slowing to 2–3 s holds in Act 3.

## C. What works, and where we can beat it

### Strengths to keep
1. **A visual concept that needs no language.** It travels across markets, with no dialogue for 70% of the film.
2. **One clean gag repeated** (the shush), which escalates through the silent siren.
3. **A single reveal** that turns spectacle into product benefit.
4. **The grade and sound contrast** carry the message: chaos is cold and muted, home is warm and silent.

### Weaknesses, which are our opportunities
| Gap in the reference | How we beat it |
|---|---|
| **Product is nearly invisible.** One blurred 1-second insert, and you can't tell uPVC from aluminium. | Make the **window the hero**: slimline black aluminium, floor-to-ceiling, the satisfying slide-and-seal close as a signature shot. Use a macro on the gasket compressing and a multipoint lock engaging. |
| **Silence is shown but not dramatised.** The window closing isn't the *cause* of the silence. | Make the close the switch. Real, loud chaos plays first, then the slide closes and the sound **vacuums out** in sync with the gasket sealing. |
| The "shh" gag is cute but passive, and stays one-note for 24 s | Escalate in tiers: chaos, then the absurd mega-chaos, then a twist ending (e.g. the robbers end up at *her* window and politely shush each other). |
| Generic, flat end card (white background, clip-art window) | A premium brand world: architectural typography, product silhouette, sonic logo built from the slide-and-click sound. |
| Speaks only to noise | Layer a second proof: noise **and** the aluminium story (slim sightlines, view, light, strength), suited to the ALCOI-style premium architect audience. |
| No sonic identity | A **sonic signature**: the soft *shhhk-thunk* of the slider sealing, used as the brand button. |

## D. Next step: invent the brand

Inputs carried forward from this review:
- **Product archetype:** a premium, factory-engineered **slimline aluminium sliding and casement system** for luxury homes and high-rises. Matte black, thermal-break, acoustic DGU/laminated glass, multipoint lock.
- **Hero benefit:** acoustic calm plus uninterrupted views ("all the view, none of the noise").
- **Naming must avoid:** ALCOI, Alu-/Sky- series names, AIS, Asahi, "noise cancelling windows" as a branded phrase.

Deliverables for stage 2: brand name, product line names, positioning, tagline, logo direction and spec sheet. After that come the treatment, script, storyboard and production plan.
