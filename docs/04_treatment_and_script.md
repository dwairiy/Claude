# PART 1: TREATMENT

## Logline
Twenty floors above a Riyadh street exploding with a blockbuster stunt, a grandfather slides one window shut so his granddaughter can write her first perfect word: **سكينة**.

## The idea
Every window commercial *says* "quiet". Ours **gives the silence a purpose**.

A master calligrapher is teaching his granddaughter to write. The word she is attempting is the hardest kind of thing to do in a noisy world, a single steady stroke. Outside their open window, the city is at full volume: a stunt-filled action film is being shot on the street below, with sirens, a superbike, a news helicopter, horns and jackhammers. Every roar makes her hand shake.

He stands, crosses to the glass and slides the SAKINA panel closed. As the gap narrows, the city drains away. On the seal, the city goes silent. In that silence she writes the word, in one flawless stroke. The word is **سكينة, *Sakina*, "tranquility"**, which is also the name of the product.

The product doesn't only block noise. It makes room for the moments that matter.

## Why this beats the reference
| Reference (AIS) | Ours |
|---|---|
| Silence is *shown*: actors shush for 24 s | Silence is **caused**: the slide and seal kill the noise in real time, on screen and on the soundtrack |
| Product on screen for about 1 s, blurred | Product is the turning point of the plot: hand on the handle, the slide, a macro of the seal, the lock |
| Generic calm payoff (meditation) | **Emotional, cultural payoff:** a generational moment, Arabic calligraphy, and a word that *is* the brand |
| Guns and a heist | **No violence.** The chaos is a professional film stunt, so it is broadcast-safe for KSA/GCC and family slots |
| Generic end card | The handwritten word turns into the product name; a sonic logo made from the product's own sound |

## Tone
**Epic, then intimate. Witty, never slapstick.** Act 1 plays like a Hollywood action trailer, shot completely straight. Act 3 plays like a quiet arthouse moment. The humour lives in the contrast. After the seal, the action outside carries on with no sound at all, like a silent film nobody inside is watching.

## Story in three movements

**I. THE CITY (0:00–0:24).** We open on a still black inkwell. Its surface trembles with the bass of the city, and we hear an alarm bell before we see anything. Then: a superbike tears through a glass business district, a patrol convoy wails after it, horns blare, jackhammers pound, and a news helicopter rises between the towers. We intercut with the apartment. A grandfather guides his granddaughter's reed pen. Wind whips the curtain through the wide-open floor-to-ceiling window. Every horn makes her line wobble, and an ink blot falls. The action builds and the crane rises. A wide shot reveals a **film crew**: cameras on cranes and a director with a megaphone. This is a stunt sequence. The rider hits a ramp and launches into the air in front of the tower, **on the music's climax**.

**II. THE SEAL (0:24–0:26).** CUT INSIDE. A weathered hand closes on a slim black handle. The 3.5 m glass panel glides. Through the narrowing gap the airborne bike is still visible, and the roar muffles. *Thunk.* A macro shot shows the interlock seal compressing. **Silence.** He turns the handle, and the multipoint lock clicks three times. Outside, the chaos carries on silently: the bike lands, crews run, the helicopter hovers. Inside there is only light.

**III. SAKINA (0:26–0:35).** The only sound is the scratch of the pen. The girl's hand is now steady and she draws one long, sweeping stroke. Her grandfather lifts his hand away and lets her finish alone. She looks up, proud, and he smiles with his eyes closed in the golden light. Behind them, through the glass, the director silently screams "CUT!" into his megaphone. A top shot shows the finished word, **سكينة**, wet ink glistening. It turns into the product name. The end card plays the sonic logo: slide, seal, chime.

## World
- **Exterior:** a fictionalised Riyadh business district with glass towers, wide boulevards, a roundabout and a construction site. Generic "traffic patrol" livery, with no real police or government marks.
- **Interior:** a Najdi-modern penthouse on the 20th floor. Warm limestone, low majlis seating, framed calligraphy, a low writing desk and an olive tree. **The SAKINA wall is the hero:** 3 panels, matte black, 20 mm interlocks, floor to ceiling.

## Visual language
| | Act 1, The City | Act 3, Sakina |
|---|---|---|
| Grade | Cool teal-steel, contrasty, crushed blacks, heat haze | Warm sand and gold, soft highlights, gentle halation |
| Camera | Long lenses, whip pans, low tracking, drone and crane, handheld energy | Locked-off or slow push, macro, 50–85 mm, shallow depth of field |
| Edit | 1 s cuts, cut to the 120 BPM grid | 2–3 s holds, no cuts during the stroke |
| Light | Hard midday sun with reflections off the glass towers | Low golden sun raking through the glass, dust in the air |

**Signature shots**
1. **The ink ripple.** City bass rippling a still inkwell (opening frame).
2. **The narrowing gap.** Foreground panel closing in on an airborne bike outside.
3. **The seal macro.** Gasket and brush seal compressing at 1000 fps.
4. **The silent film.** The family in the foreground, a wordless circus outside the glass behind them.
5. **Ink to identity.** The written word **سكينة** turns into the SAKINA logotype.

## Sound
Already composed (doc 03): *"Rush Hour Heist"* (D minor) gives way to silence, then *"Sakina"* (D major) and the sonic logo. Revisions needed for this script are listed at the end of Part 2.

## Compliance
- No weapons, no crime, no violence. The stunt is clearly a film production. Super: *"Professional stunt. Controlled film set."*
- No real police or government livery; generic "patrol" vehicles.
- Child talent: a guardian on set, plus GCC child-talent rules on working hours.
- Acoustic claim super: *"Up to Rw 42 dB in tested configuration. Results vary by installation."* (Product is fictional, for portfolio use.)
- Broadcast master at −23 LUFS (EBU R128).

## Production approach (recommendation)
| Option | Fit | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Hybrid (recommended)** | AI or CG for the exterior chaos (Nano Banana stills, then image-to-video). **Shoot the interior inserts for real:** hands, pen, paper, window slide and seal macro, in one day in an apartment with a slider. | Matching light between the AI and live footage; solved with the grade |
| B. Fully AI | Fastest and cheapest | **Arabic calligraphy cannot be AI-generated reliably; the letters break.** Character consistency is also weak across shots. |
| C. Full live action | Highest craft | Stunts, permits and a helicopter make it a multi-day budget |

**Non-negotiable:** the word سكينة must be written by a **real calligrapher** (Thuluth or Diwani script), filmed or scanned and composited. This is the emotional core and the portfolio's proof of craft.

### Voiceover
- **Casting:** a warm, mature male voice, unhurried and intimate (the grandfather's spirit, but not his on-screen voice). The English version uses the same register.
- **Arabic (master), 27.0–32.4 s:**
  > في الخارج… العالم لا يهدأ.
  > وفي الداخل… سكينة.
  > من المهيب.
- **English, 27.0–32.4 s:**
  > Outside, the world never stops.
  > Inside… SAKINA.
  > By AL Mohib.
- The double meaning is the heart of the line: *sakina* is both "tranquility" and the product name.
